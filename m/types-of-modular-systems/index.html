☆======= Types of modular systems =======☆

Around my time messing with modular systems, researching and learning about them closely i developed a bit of a line between two types of "modular" and decided to give it names and state them here.
- Chain Modularity

This type represents the generalized thought of a modular system; it is a system composed by multiple little parts, each with their own task or way of handling information, toguether they form a system. This is used to just offload lots of hardwork into a single system in the development: easy to mantain, upgrade, implement stuff and handling errors. I gave it the name "Chain" for the simple reason of a chain connection things, when a part of the chain breaks, the whole thing falls and one end doesn't connect to the other end. Fancy?

- Free Modularity

About the same as chain modularity, but this time each module (little parts of the system) actually can either work toguether with other modules or work alone by themselves. Like a Lego.. but if you remove that module, everything keeps working together without falling down, just without that specific piece. Each module is not crucial or dependent on anything - it's just an extra.

Im aware that are concepts out there that kind of deal with this prespective, other types of modularity... this is a heavily generalized and oversimplified overview of my way of understanding modularity. Also aware that i didn't invent or discovered something here, just stating and pushing this concept out there just because i think is cool.
